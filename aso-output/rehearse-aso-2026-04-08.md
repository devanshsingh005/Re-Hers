## Step 1: /app-marketing-context

### 1. Score / Status

Status: Complete. Positioning direction: strong, but the current metadata under-sells the product's most differentiated workflow.

### 2. Key Findings

- ReHearse's strongest promise is not "sheet music library"; competitors already own that. The sharper position is "turn my own sheet music into readable notes and a practice guide."
- The scan -> labeled English notes -> animated playback -> play-along -> chord feedback chain is distinct from MuseScore/Musicnotes library browsing, forScore PDF management, and Simply Piano structured lessons.
- The current description says "Music Rehearse Studio" instead of "ReHearse," which weakens brand consistency.
- The audience should be segmented by confidence with notation: beginner/self-learner, student, hobbyist returner, teacher, and ensemble player.
- Monetization should make scanning/basic note labels free, then unlock deeper playback, premium library content, chord recognition, export, and practice tools.

### 3. Deliverable

#### Marketing Context

ReHearse helps musicians turn static sheet music into an interactive practice session. A user scans a page, sees labeled English notes, watches an animated guide move through the music, then plays along with feedback and chord recognition.

#### Unique Differentiators

- Own-sheet-music workflow: not only a catalog or lesson course, but a way to practice music the user already has.
- English note labeling: helpful for learners who struggle to map staff notation to note names.
- Animated playback guide: makes sheet music feel less static and more followable.
- Play-along practice mode: bridges the gap between reading and performing.
- Chord recognition: gives feedback that most sheet music readers and libraries do not center.
- Teacher utility: can support assignments, demonstrations, and independent student practice.

#### Competitor Contrast

- MuseScore: strongest on massive sheet music catalog, community, playback, and chords. ReHearse should not compete on library size first.
- forScore: strongest for serious iPad sheet music management, annotation, and performance workflows. ReHearse should compete on learning assistance and scan-to-practice.
- Musicnotes: strongest on licensed sheet music purchasing and official arrangements. ReHearse should compete on transforming existing sheets.
- Simply Piano: strongest on beginner piano courses and real-time feedback. ReHearse should compete on any scanned music, not a fixed curriculum.
- Pianist HD / piano simulator apps: strongest on playable keyboard/game experience. ReHearse should compete on sheet-to-practice conversion and notation support.

#### Audience Segments

- Music students: "I need to practice assigned sheet music between lessons."
- Self-learners: "I want to learn a song without a teacher."
- Hobbyists and returning musicians: "I used to read music, but I need help getting back into it."
- Beginners who struggle with notation: "I can't read sheet music quickly enough to play."
- Teachers: "I want students to practice more efficiently at home."
- Ensemble/church/community musicians: "I need to rehearse a part before group practice."

#### Jobs To Be Done

- I want to learn a song without a teacher.
- I can't read sheet music confidently.
- I want to practice efficiently, not just stare at a page.
- I want my own sheet music to become easier to follow.
- I want feedback when I play chords.
- I want to prepare for lessons, rehearsals, recitals, or worship sets.

#### Monetization Assumptions

- Freemium acquisition:
  - Free: scan a limited number of pages, see note labels, basic playback preview, access sample library.
  - Premium: unlimited scans, full animated playback, play-along mode, chord recognition, premium library, saved practice sessions, advanced exports, teacher/student sharing.
  - Conversion hooks: scan limit reached, advanced playback required, chord feedback locked, premium score selected.

#### Growth Goals

- Rank top 10 for "sheet music" and "music practice" over time.
- Near-term realistic target: rank for lower-competition intent phrases such as "sheet music scanner," "read music notes," "music practice app," "play along sheet music," and "chord recognition."
- Conversion goal: make screenshot 1 and first three description lines instantly explain scan -> notes -> play along.

### 4. Next Action

Validate monetization and top audience priority before final store submission. If ReHearse is piano-only today, narrow the metadata to piano terms; if it supports more instruments, keep the broader musician positioning.
